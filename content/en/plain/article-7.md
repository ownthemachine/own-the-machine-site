L0: Guarantees court review of the valuation and that shareholders never lose more than the fixed 3 %.

This article states the limits and the remedies. A valuation can be challenged in court on its own, without having to attack the designation or the deal it relates to: before the courts of the EU country where the company is registered, or before the Court of Justice of the European Union if the company has no registered office in the EU. Whatever happens, shareholders lose no more than the 3 % share of the company set in Article 5, measured on the valuation as corrected by the courts where needed. Where a company has split its automated assets among one or more transferees under Article 5(11), that 3 % is still the total: it applies to the combined dilution suffered by the shareholders of the original company and of every transferee together, not 3 % apiece. Rights to compensation from anyone other than the Reserve under national law stay untouched.
