L0: Makes the Commission track the evidence and propose amendment or repeal if the rules' own premise proves wrong.

Using official statistics, the Commission must track the spread of automated systems among companies, household share ownership, the labour and capital shares of value added and the Regulation's own results, including designations and payouts. By 31 December 2032, and every three years after, it must evaluate the Regulation and publish its report. The report must state whether the evidence still supports the premise that gains from automation flow mainly to those who own the systems. If the evidence points the other way, it must say so expressly and, where appropriate, propose amending or repealing the rules. Proposals touching the core articles need an independent assessment of their effect on holders.
