L0: Sets out what the rules do and who they cover: highly automated companies selling in the EU, wherever they are based.

This article sets out the plan of the whole Regulation. It creates a procedure for deciding which companies are covered and obliges those companies to issue a citizens' capital warrant (a right to future shares) to a new European fund, the Reserve. It establishes the Reserve itself and the rules on how it holds and manages what it receives. It gives EU citizens a personal right to payments from the Reserve, administered through bodies in each country, and it protects both the fund and that right. The rules apply to any company offering goods or services in the EU market, no matter where in the world it is based.
