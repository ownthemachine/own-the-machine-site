L0: Standard machinery: a committee of national representatives oversees the Commission's implementing decisions.

This is standard machinery, the routine committee article found in most EU regulations. When the Commission takes implementing decisions under these rules, it is assisted by a committee of representatives of the Member States, operating under the EU's general comitology rules (Regulation (EU) No 182/2011). In this Regulation its role concerns the implementing decisions assigned to the Commission, notably establishing the list of independent valuers and appointing them under Article 6. Where the examination procedure applies, the committee votes on the Commission's draft measures before they are adopted. The article changes nothing in the substance of the rules; it controls how those decisions are taken.
