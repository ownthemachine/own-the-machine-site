L0: Puts an independent expert in charge of valuing the company at each trigger event, including early crystallisation for heavy payouts or the seven-year long-stop, with errors corrected either way.

The number of shares the Reserve receives depends on the company's total share capital, counted with every convertible and option included (the fully diluted capital), valued immediately before the triggering event: the sale, listing or change of control, or, where the warrant instead crystallised early because payouts to the company's owners were too high or seven years had passed, immediately before that crystallisation date. That count is verified by a valuer who is independent of the company, of the Reserve and of everyone involved in the deal, appointed by the Commission from a list built through an open call. Where a deal itself establishes a price, the valuation is based on that price. It is delivered within 20 working days of the deal completing, or of the crystallisation date, and the company bears the cost. If a court later finds the value was overstated or understated, the share count is corrected in either direction. A challenge never suspends the deal or the crystallisation itself.
