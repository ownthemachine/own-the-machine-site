L0: Puts an independent expert in charge of valuing the company at each trigger event, with errors corrected either way.

The number of shares the Reserve receives depends on the company's total share capital, counted with every convertible and option included (the fully diluted capital), immediately before the sale or listing. That count is verified by a valuer who is independent of the company, of the Reserve and of everyone involved in the deal, appointed by the Commission from a list built through an open call. Where the deal itself establishes a price, the valuation is based on that price. It is delivered within 20 working days of the deal completing, and the company bears the cost. If a court later finds the value was overstated or understated, the share count is corrected in either direction. A challenge never suspends the deal itself.
