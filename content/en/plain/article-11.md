L0: Makes every EU country run personal accounts that receive the payouts, with charges capped at 0,3 % a year.

Each EU country must, within 12 months, designate a national vehicle: the body that keeps an individual account in your name and credits your payouts to it. Existing pension institutions may take the role. The vehicle must pass distributions on without deductions other than permitted charges, treat your balance as your property and ask nothing of you beyond proof of identity, age and citizenship. Its charges may not exceed its actual costs and never 0,3 % a year of the amounts it administers. If your country provides no working vehicle, you may register with one in another EU country, which may not refuse you because of where you live; your own country must then reimburse that vehicle for any verifiable extra cost above the charge ceiling.
