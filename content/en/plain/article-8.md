L0: Creates the Reserve: a fund holding the shares only for citizens, never as government money, paying out what it can spare.

The European Citizens' Capital Reserve is the fund that receives the warrants and the shares they turn into. It holds everything solely for the people entitled to payments. Its assets and income are not government money: they never enter the EU budget or any national budget, and no public budget pays into or takes from it. Each year it keeps back enough of its realised income to preserve the real value of its capital and makes the rest available for distribution; it may not borrow or sell holdings just to fund a payout. It publishes audited accounts, the value of its holdings and the retention calculation every year.
