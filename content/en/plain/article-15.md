L0: Standard machinery: controls how the Commission may update two technical annexes, under Parliament and Council oversight.

This is the EU's standard machinery for delegated acts, applied here to two narrow technical powers: updating the methodology for counting turnover, workers and value (Annex I) and the methodology for the Reserve's annual retention (Annex II). The Commission holds these powers for five years at a time, renewed automatically unless the European Parliament or the Council objects. Either institution can revoke a power at any moment. Before adopting an act the Commission must consult national experts, and an adopted act takes effect only if neither Parliament nor Council objects within two months, extendable by two more. The headline thresholds cannot be changed this way.
