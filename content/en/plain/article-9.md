L0: Forbids the Reserve from voting its shares, steering companies, borrowing, lending or speculating.

The Reserve is a silent owner. It may never vote the shares it holds, take seats on company boards or give instructions to companies, formally or informally. It may only acquire stakes through the warrants, by reinvesting its own income or by prudently spreading holdings it has received. It may not borrow, except briefly for cash management up to 2 % of its assets, or to pay for shares it is subscribing under a warrant, which it must then repay out of the first income it later earns. It may not make loans or give guarantees and may not use derivatives (contracts betting on prices) except to hedge currency risk on what it holds. Its sole objective is the long-term preservation and growth of value for the people entitled to payments.
