L0: Requires initial notifications and leaves completed past events outside the warrant.

Undertakings already meeting the thresholds notify within two months of entry into force. A listing before entry into force does not itself trigger the warrant; later qualifying events and the extraction or seven-year rules can do so. Events completed before general application create no Article 5 obligation. The revised clock and effective-designation rules apply under Articles 5 and 18.
