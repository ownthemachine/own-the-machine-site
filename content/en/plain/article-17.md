L0: Startup rules: past deals stay untouched and a listing that already happened does not trigger the share purchase.

These rules only reach forward, never back. A company already above the thresholds when the Regulation enters into force must report itself within two months of that date. If a company's shares were already listed on a stock exchange before the Regulation existed, that listing does not count as the trigger event: its warrant waits, dormant, until the first change of control or qualifying sale of at least 20 % of its shares within 12 months occurring after its designation, or until it crystallises early under the heavy-extraction or seven-year long-stop rule, whichever happens first. An already-listed company gets no exemption from those two rules. And a sale or listing completed before the Regulation entered into force creates no obligation at all.
