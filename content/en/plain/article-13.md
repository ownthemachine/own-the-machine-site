L0: Makes execution and reporting enforceable, including for bound transferees.

Intentional or negligent breaches of the listed issuance, subscription, reporting, information and avoidance duties can attract fines up to 10 % of worldwide turnover. Bound transferees are expressly included. Daily penalties can reach 5 % of average daily turnover. Persistent issuance or subscription default by an undertaking outside the Union can lead to a last-resort, temporary market prohibition. The Commission must hear the undertaking and consider gravity, duration and recurrence. EU courts can review the penalties. Revenue goes to the Union budget, not the Reserve.
