L0: Lets the Commission fine companies up to 10 % of worldwide turnover and, as a last resort, bar them from the EU market.

A company that fails to issue the warrant, deliver the shares or notify a sale or listing, supplies false information, dodges designation through an artificial arrangement, or ignores a Commission decision about such an arrangement, can be fined up to 10 % of its worldwide turnover. To compel compliance the Commission may add daily penalties of up to 5 % of average daily worldwide turnover. A company based outside the EU that persistently refuses to comply can, as a last resort and only while the refusal lasts, be barred from offering the relevant goods and services in the EU. The EU courts can review, cut or raise every penalty. Fines go to the EU's general budget, never to the Reserve.
