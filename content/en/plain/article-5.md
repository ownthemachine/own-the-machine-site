L0: Establishes the share claim at designation, with subscription at a statutory trigger.

The warrant arises when designation takes effect; the company records it within three months. A listing, change of control or qualifying 20 % share transfer after designation can trigger it even before that document is issued. It also crystallises if extraction exceeds 25 % of covered turnover over three consecutive financial years, or after seven years from the claim arising. Those last two triggers take effect at the relevant financial year-end. The Reserve subscribes for newly issued shares measured at 3 % of pre-event fully diluted capital and pays their nominal value in cash. Execution follows independent valuation within 20 working days.

The warrant confers no votes or management rights. Its ranking and anti-subordination protections preserve earlier preferences and the specified rescue exception. Reporting, valuation and anti-avoidance duties apply before subscription. Bound asset transferees owe warrants too; they do not restart the clock, and the aggregate cap counts shares already subscribed. Genuine rescue by unconnected buyers remains outside the same-owner reattachment rule. Article 5 supplies the detailed conditions.
