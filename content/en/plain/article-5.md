L0: Requires each covered company to give the Reserve the right to buy 3 % of its shares cheaply when it is first sold or listed.

Within three months of designation, a designated company must issue the citizens' capital warrant to the Reserve. The warrant stays dormant until the company's first listing, change of control or qualifying share sale. Then the Reserve may buy newly issued shares equal to 3 % of the company's capital, paying only their nominal value (a token price). Neither the warrant nor the shares it turns into carry votes or any say in management for as long as the Reserve holds them; the warrant cannot be settled in cash or transferred; until the event, the company's only duty is to give notice of an impending one. The purchase is completed within 20 working days of the independent valuation, and dilution can never exceed the 3 %.
