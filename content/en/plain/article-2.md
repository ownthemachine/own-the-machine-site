L0: Defines the key terms: which companies count, what the warrant is and which events turn it into shares.

This article fixes the meaning of the words the rest of the rules rely on. A 'covered undertaking' is a designated company, counted together with its whole corporate group. 'Automated cognitive services' are services performed largely by software doing work that would otherwise need human thinking. The 'citizens' capital warrant' is the instrument giving the Reserve the right to buy new shares in a designated company. A 'liquidity event', the moment the warrant turns into shares, is a stock-market listing, a change of control or sales adding up to at least 20 % of the company's shares within 12 months. The article imposes no obligations by itself.
