L0: Creates legal barriers against the EU or governments raiding the fund or your share, with a court remedy if anyone tries.

This article raises the legal barriers. No act of the EU or a national government may cancel, write down, seize, transfer, suspend, lend out or pledge the Reserve's assets or your entitlement for the benefit of any public budget. The Reserve is also barred from holding government bonds or any debt guaranteed by the EU, a state, a local authority or a central bank, so there is nothing for a treasury to swap out. The Regulation cannot be read as authorising any exception. If any of this is breached, you, and the national vehicle acting for its holders, can go to court and demand an effective remedy.
