L0: Lets the Commission investigate borderline companies, recheck the list regularly and remove companies that no longer qualify.

The Commission may investigate whether a company below the automatic thresholds should be covered; the investigation must end within 12 months. Every designation is reviewed at least every three years, or earlier when the company shows the facts have substantially changed. A designation is withdrawn when the company has not met the conditions for two consecutive financial years, unless that results from an avoidance arrangement. Withdrawal does not undo a warrant already issued, but a warrant that has not yet turned into shares lapses five years after withdrawal if no trigger event has occurred. Before any decision, the company gets at least 20 working days to be heard.
