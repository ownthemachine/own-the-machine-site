L0: Standard machinery: the rules become law 20 days after publication and apply in full 18 months later.

This is the standard closing article of every EU regulation. The Regulation enters into force on the twentieth day after its publication in the EU's Official Journal. Its obligations then start applying 18 months later, a delay that gives the designation machinery and the national account bodies time to be built. Three duties apply immediately from entry into force: companies' duty to notify that they meet the thresholds, Member States' duty to designate national vehicles and the same duty for companies already above the thresholds. As a regulation it is binding in its entirety and applies directly in every Member State, without needing national laws to implement it.
