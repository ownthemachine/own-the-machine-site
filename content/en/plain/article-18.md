L0: Starts preparation early and substantive obligations 18 months after entry into force.

The Regulation enters into force 20 days after publication. Specified notification, designation, hearing, appointment and institutional preparation provisions apply then. Designation decisions made during preparation take effect at general application, 18 months after entry into force. The warrant and its clock start no earlier; completed earlier liquidity events are not captured. The initial valuation appointment arrangements must be ready by that date. The Regulation is directly applicable in every Member State.
