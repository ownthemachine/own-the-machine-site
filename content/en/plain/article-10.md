L0: Gives every adult EU citizen an equal automatic share of the payouts, which cannot be sold, seized or signed away.

If you are an EU citizen aged 18 or over, you hold this entitlement: a personal right to an equal share of every payout the Reserve declares. It arises automatically. There is no application and no test of income, wealth, work or contribution history; you only register with a national vehicle (the body in your country that runs the accounts) so you can be paid. Every holder's share is equal. The right itself cannot be sold, pledged, seized or surrendered, but money already credited to your account is your property and can be inherited. The Reserve must declare a payout at least once in any year its income allows one.
