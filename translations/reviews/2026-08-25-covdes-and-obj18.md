# Covered/designated resolved, and the objection 18 delta (25 August 2026)

Two changes to the objections translations, both driven by the law repo.

## The covered/designated fork, closed

The last known terminology fork: the objections memorandum rendered
*covered undertaking* with each language's word for *designated*, while the
rest of the corpus already distinguished them. Twenty-eight spots fixed
(nl 8 including the objection 5 title, fr 8, de 4, es 8), each verified
against the English first; the twenty-odd genuine designation references
stand. Fidelity smoke on the most-edited stretch: no BLOCKING; both REVISE
findings predate the change.

## The objection 18 delta, and what its gate found

The rescue carve-out of Article 5(10) settled a question objection 18 had
carried as open, so the four translations received the new answer and the
DC-36 qualifier. The fidelity round on that one objection then caught, in
one pass:

- **The paragraph inversion fixed in German on 23 August existed
  identically in Dutch, French and Spanish** and had never been caught
  there, because that round's finding was read as a German defect rather
  than as a question to ask of the other three. Fixed as the same pure
  block swap. The lesson is recorded here because it is general: when a
  gate finds a structural defect in one language, the other translations
  are guilty until checked.
- The Article 5(10) main-purpose-or-effect standard, restored in German and
  Spanish in the chunk D round, was also missing in Dutch and French.
- One of mine: the Spanish DC-36 *table* row received the carve-out
  qualifier while the *prose* line did not, because the patch targeted the
  table twice and the prose not at all.

Second pass: zero BLOCKING in all four languages.
