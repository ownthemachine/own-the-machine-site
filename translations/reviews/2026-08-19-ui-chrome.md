# UI chrome, all languages: the untranslated remainders

Reported by David from the French ledger page: "On this page" rendered
in English on every localized page (the OnPage component hardcoded the
strings although every dictionary already carried onThisPage and
backToTop), and the ledger showed the enOnly banner ("this page is not
yet available in French") above a page whose prose is French. A sweep
of every template for hardcoded visible text found these two plus the
404 page; brand names (GitHub, PDF, EPUB) and the law reader's
"Whereas:" (part of the authentic English text) stay by design.

The ledger banner was replaced by an accurate one: the reviews are
conducted in English and the entries quote their records in the
original; the rest of the page is translated. New key
banner.recordsEnglish in the five dictionaries.

Banner gate round 1 (REVISE x4): all four reviewers correctly flagged
that the "rest of this page" clause existed only in the translations;
the clause was the banner's point, so it was added to the English
source rather than removed. Naturalness fixes applied per language
(vinden plaats / nemen over in de oorspronkelijke taal; évaluations /
comptes rendus / en version originale; finden statt / nachstehende;
se realizan / registros / en su idioma original). Round 2: PUBLISH x4.
