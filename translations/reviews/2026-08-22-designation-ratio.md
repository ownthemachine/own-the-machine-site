# Editor disposition (22 August 2026, evening)

The passages carrying the designation-ratio package into the four
languages: the rewritten objection 21 answer, the objection 17 incidence
paragraph, and the plain summaries of articles 1 and 3, which changed
meaning and were rewritten rather than patched.

## The finding that mattered

Spanish rendered "citizens' capital warrant" in the plain summary of
Article 1 as *bono de capital ciudadano*. A *bono* is a bond, a debt
security; the warrant is an equity instrument, and the same page's
objection 17 already uses *warrant* consistently. BLOCKING, and rightly:
mischaracterising the instrument's legal nature in the plainest summary
on the site, in one language, is precisely the class of error that
survives every structural check because every count matches.

## The pattern find

German caught a category error in my new incidence sentence: companies
cannot *umfassen* (comprise) countries. The same construction existed in
the Dutch (*omvatten*), unflagged there, and was fixed in both; the
English "span" survives because English tolerates the metonymy. Third
instance of the session's rule: an error produced by one construction
appears in every language sharing the gap, so one language's finding is a
search instruction for the others.

## Older text improved in passing

Because the bundle carried the whole of objection 17 for context, the
gates reviewed its pre-existing translations for the first time and found
real defects that predate today: French *expropriation par règlement* for
"expropriation by regulation" (which in investment law means regulatory
expropriation, not expropriation by an EU regulation - BLOCKING),
*filiale mince* for "thin subsidiary", Dutch *zou verdienen te
verliezen*, German *die Gegenseitigkeit arbeitet für*. All applied. The
accidental widening of the bundle turned out to be the most productive
part of the round, which argues for occasionally re-bundling old
sections rather than only ever gating the diff.

## Residue

Native-ear REVISE persists on naturalness preferences across all four
languages, the normal terminal state for new copy. No unapplied finding
alleges a change of meaning.
