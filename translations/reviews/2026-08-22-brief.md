# Editor disposition (22 August 2026)

The one-page brief, translated into four languages. Twenty-eight fixes.

## Three of these were not translation defects, they were legal errors

**German and Spanish both dropped the citizenship requirement.** The English
says every adult *citizen* holds the entitlement. The German said *jede
erwachsene Person* and the Spanish *toda persona adulta*, both of which
extend the entitlement to every adult resident of the Union. On the page
whose entire job is to be handed to a policy officer, in a document about a
Citizens' Capital Reserve, in two of the five languages. Neither the
structural check nor the one-page check could see it: the counts matched and
it printed on one sheet.

**Spanish used a tax verb in the paragraph arguing that this is not a tax.**
*Grava un activo* is the standard verb for levying a tax. The paragraph
exists to answer the charge that the instrument is a tax under Article 113
and therefore needs unanimity, and it was making that charge for the reader
in Spanish. It now reads *recae sobre un activo*.

**Dutch narrowed the scope of designation.** *Hypergeautomatiseerde
productie* adds "production" to a threshold the English states without it,
which reads as excluding services, finance and software. That is most of the
undertakings this instrument is aimed at.

Two further errors were grammatical rather than substantive but would have
been visible to any native reader: a dangling participle in the Dutch and a
missing passive auxiliary in the German, both in the same sentence about the
objection being stated and answered.

## A terminology collision only Spanish had

Spanish rendered both "the warrant" and "the entitlement" as *el derecho*,
so the corporate instrument and the civic right had the same name in a
four-bullet list that exists to distinguish them. Now *el derecho de
suscripción* and *la titularidad*.

## On the one-page claim

The page promises one sheet of A4. That promise lived in a stylesheet, so
check-brief.mjs prints it in all five languages with a headless browser and
counts the page objects in the PDF.

It earned its place immediately. The first version of this page ran to two
sheets in every language. The first three attempts to fix that moved it by
twelve pixels, because base.css sets font-size and line-height on
`.prose p, .prose li` rather than on the container, so sizing the container
did nothing at all. The fourth attempt overshot in the other direction and
produced a brief that fitted on one page by using only 60 % of it in 8.5pt
type, which is a worse document than a two-pager. It now sets 10.5pt on the
children and fills the sheet: 802px of 1025 in English, 888px in German,
which is the longest.

The check was itself falsified before being trusted. The first attempt to
prove it caught a spill added one paragraph, the check passed, and the
honest reading was not that the check works but that the test was too weak:
there was 140px of slack and the paragraph was 100px. With three sections
added it fails on German and passes again when they are removed.
