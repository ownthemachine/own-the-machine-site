# Editor disposition (23 August 2026)

Three new citizen-facing pages in five languages: the FAQ that answers what
people actually ask, the press kit, and the how-signing-works page. These
close the largest citizen-comprehension gap the 22 August audit found, and
the FAQ carries the DC-14 disclosure (payments start near zero and take a
generation) in the first substantive answer.

## The shared-gap rule, fourth confirmation

The English sentence "anyone collecting data for this initiative today is
not us" produced ungrammatical output in Dutch (*is ons niet*) and German
(*ist nicht wir*), both BLOCKING. Same construction, same gap, two
languages, exactly as with *wetgeeft*/*gesetzgebt* on the objections pages.
The rule holds: an English construction with no equivalent in one target
grammar will break every language sharing the gap, so one finding is a
search instruction for the rest.

## Grammar the author missed twice

French *si vous envisageriez*: a conditional after hypothetical *si* is
ungrammatical, and it appeared in both the FAQ and the signing page,
written the same evening by the same hand. BLOCKING, fixed in both.

## Meaning errors worth recording

- Spanish rendered "an equivalent cash-out" as *una caja equivalente*,
  which is nonsense in financial prose, and dropped the currency from the
  EUR 7,5 billion threshold. Both in the press kit, the one page
  journalists copy verbatim.
- Spanish read "above this page" as *sobre esta página*, about this page:
  a spatial/topical ambiguity that reversed the sentence's function.
- French *enregistrabilité* is not a word the EU uses; the request is now
  described as one on the conditions of registration.
- German advice to citizens to *greifen Sie es an* reads as incitement
  rather than "argue with it"; now *widersprechen Sie ihm*.

## Refused: none

Every fidelity finding was applied. The native-ear residue is naturalness
preference at the terminal level normal for new copy.
