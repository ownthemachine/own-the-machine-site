# Editor disposition (22 August 2026)

The take-part page, translated into four languages and put through both
gates. Twenty-three fixes applied across the four.

## Why this page exists

Gate 2 needs seven organisers in seven Member States plus two institutional
partners, and until today a reader convinced by the objections page had one
route out of it: an email address in the footer of a different page. The
campaign's declared bottleneck had no path on the site that led to it.

The page is deliberately not a sign-up. Nothing is collected, there is no
form, no list and no third-party anything, so the privacy position stated on
the about page survives intact. What it does instead is tell a reader what
being an organiser actually involves, what it does not commit them to, and
that the public route is better than the private one.

## Refused, with reasons

**Renaming the gates in French.** The reviewer called *portes publiées* a
calque of "published gates" and proposed *jalons publics*. The about page has
said *portes de contrôle* since it was written, so the fix was to use the
term already in the file rather than either the calque or the reviewer's
third option. Same class of defect as the *reviewpoorten* error yesterday,
and the same resolution.

**Softening "adversarial" in German.** Twice: *feindselige Begutachtung* and
*feindselige Lektüre*, both proposed to become *schonungslose Prüfung*. The
reviewer is right that *feindselig* carries personal enmity, which is not
what the source means, and wrong that the answer is to drop the adversary.
This project's method is that someone argues against the text on purpose.
Both now read *gegnerisch*, which is the adversarial sense without the
malice.

## Genuinely caught

A Dutch BLOCKING grammar error: *niemand die het in het openbaar oneens is*
is broken, because *oneens zijn* needs its prepositional object. Also a
German ungrammatical *nichts zustimmen*, a German quotation mark closed with
an ASCII quote instead of the high closing quote, and in Spanish an
inconsistency where the same page said *elecciones europeas* in one place and
*elecciones al Parlamento Europeo* in another.

## A defect in the checker, found by writing this page

Every localised page in this repository carried unprefixed internal links, so
a Dutch reader clicking through to the versions page landed on the English
one, silently, mid-sentence. The prefix is a property of where a page is
served, not of what a translator wrote, so sync-law.mjs now applies it at
render time and asset paths are left alone. That fixed the about pages in
four languages as a side effect.

## Residue

Both gates still return REVISE on naturalness preferences, which is the
terminal state for newly written copy and why a written disposition is what
the governance rule asks for. No round-one finding alleged a change of
meaning that was not applied.
