# Native read, Dutch: first pass

The pipeline's top tier: a native reader's findings, recorded like any
other review.

1. "Niets is betaalbaar in geld": in Dutch financial and legal usage,
   payable in cash is "in contanten"; "niet opeisbaar in contanten"
   reads more idiomatically than "niet betaalbaar in geld".
   Accepted: "Niets is opeisbaar in contanten".
2. "enige overheidsbegroting": "enige" can read as a calque of English
   "any"; "de staatskas" is stronger and more natural.
   Accepted: "niets vloeit naar de staatskas".
