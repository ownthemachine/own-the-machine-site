# Objection 18 in four languages, and an honest lag

Objection 18 (corporate structuring) and the six design consequences it
brought were translated and gated. The gate did something more useful
than pass them.

## What the gate caught in the source

Its first run reported that all four translations diverged from the
English on three points: the extraction trigger, the ranking rule and
the succession rule. The translations were right and the English was
stale. Objection 18 had been written against the first draft of the
articles, hostile counsel had then improved all three provisions, and
the memorandum's prose was never brought along. It described a 15 %
fair-market-value trigger that had become 25 % of covered turnover, a
ranking against the most favourable class in issue that had become
equality with classes created after designation, and a pro-rata
attachment that had become a warrant of the transferee's own. Fixed in
the law repository at c06c2e8. A translation gate is not supposed to
audit the source text; this one did.

## What is knowingly left undone

The second run surfaced the translation debt of the whole day: the
passages added to objection 1 (James and Others v United Kingdom, the
Article 345 addressee answer, the honest statement of what BRRD is
borrowed for), the paragraph added to objection 6 on valuation
litigation, the realignment of objection 9, and the status note. Those
are English-only for now.

Rather than rush thirty-three items at the end of a long working day,
the four pages keep source-commit at cb3da17. The consequence is
deliberate and visible: every translated objections page now carries
the staleness banner telling the reader that the English original has
moved on and that details may differ until the translation is updated.
That is what the mechanism is for, and it is the truthful state of the
pages.

Next translation task, in order: objection 1's three additions,
objection 6's paragraph, objection 9's realignment, the status note.
When they are done the source-commit moves and the banner clears.
