### Finding 1: Overclaim (DC-23 Raid Resistance Framing)

* **Legal Text (Article 12(1), (3)):**
  > "1. The assets of the Reserve and the entitlements of holders shall not be cancelled, written down, compulsorily redeemed, seized, transferred, suspended, lent, encumbered or otherwise applied, in whole or in part, for the benefit of the general budget of the Union, of any Member State or of any public body, by any act of the Union or of a Member State."  
  > "3. This Regulation shall not be interpreted as authorising any authority of the Union or of a Member State to derogate from paragraphs 1 and 2."
* **Plain-Language Layer (Article 12 L0 & Body):**
  > "L0: Blocks the EU and governments from raiding the fund or your share of it..."  
  > "This article is the lock. Nothing the EU or a national government does may cancel, write down, seize, transfer, suspend, lend out or pledge the Reserve's assets or your entitlement for the benefit of any public budget."
* **Reader Harm:** Violates DC-23 by presenting raid resistance as absolute legal impossibility ("the lock", "blocks") rather than institutional and procedural friction backed by judicial review. Because subsequent primary or secondary legislation can amend EU regulations (as expressly contemplated in Article 14(4)), describing the statutory prohibition as an unbreakable lock misinforms citizens about the constitutional limits of statutory safeguards.
* **Minimal Fix (Plain Layer – Article 12):** Replace "This article is the lock" and absolute blocking language with phrasing describing legal prohibitions, structural separation of assets, and judicial remedies (e.g., *"Creates legal and financial barriers against public budgets raiding the fund, and gives you a court remedy if anyone tries."*).

### Finding 2: Underdisclosure (Non-Voting Status of Subscribed Shares)

* **Legal Text (Article 5(3)(a)):**
  > "(a) confer no voting rights, no rights of information beyond those provided in this Regulation and no right to participate in the management of the covered undertaking, and the shares subscribed pursuant to it shall be non-voting for as long as they are held by the Reserve;"
* **Plain-Language Layer (Article 5 Body):**
  > "The warrant carries no votes and no say in management, cannot be settled in cash or transferred; until the event, the company's only duty is to give notice of an impending one."
* **Reader Harm:** The plain summary discloses that the *warrant* carries no voting rights, but omits the crucial statutory limitation that the *underlying shares* issued upon crystallisation remain strictly non-voting as long as the Reserve holds them. Citizens and market participants could believe the Reserve assumes active shareholder voting power post-crystallisation.
* **Minimal Fix (Plain Layer – Article 5):** Amend the sentence in the Article 5 plain layer to: *"Neither the warrant nor the shares it turns into carry voting rights or any say in management for as long as the Reserve holds them..."*

### Finding 3: Term Drift / Conceptual Drift ("Fully Diluted Capital" vs. "Company Value")

* **Legal Text (Article 5(2), Article 6(2), (4)):**
  > "Article 5(2): ...subscribe at nominal value for newly issued shares representing 3 % of the fully diluted capital of the covered undertaking determined immediately before that event."  
  > "Article 6(2): The valuation shall determine the fully diluted capital of the covered undertaking immediately before the liquidity event..."
* **Plain-Language Layer (Article 6 Body):**
  > "The number of shares the Reserve receives depends on the company's value immediately before the sale or listing. That value is set by a valuer who is independent of the company..."
* **Reader Harm:** Conflates the company's financial valuation (enterprise/equity market value) with its "fully diluted capital" (the total share capital base/diluted share count). The Reserve is entitled to a fixed 3 % of the fully diluted share count, not a variable volume of shares derived from an appraisal of company cash worth. Stating that the number of shares depends on the company's financial value confuses readers regarding how dilution and share allocation operate.
* **Minimal Fix (Plain Layer – Article 6):** Update Article 6 plain layer to state: *"The number of shares the Reserve receives depends on the company's total fully diluted share capital immediately before the sale or listing. That share count is verified by an independent valuer..."*
